# Privacy

Henry's ApC-Pdf Convertor processes completed AP Classroom assignments locally in your Chrome browser.

- The extension does not send assignment text, answers, scores, or generated PDFs to a server.
- It does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, browsing history, cookies, credentials, or account profile details.
- It reads the assignment only after you click **Read assignment**.
- AP Classroom pages use your existing site login. Figures are retrieved from the named Learnosity asset host and embedded locally.
- Question content is held in the review tab's memory. Closing the tab clears it.
- Session storage records only the IDs needed to close temporary reading tabs. It is not synchronized to other devices.
- A PDF is saved only after you confirm the preview and start generation. You choose the save location.
- Published code, tests, and release archives contain no real assignment questions or student data.

No external processing service is used. The project is independent of College Board.
